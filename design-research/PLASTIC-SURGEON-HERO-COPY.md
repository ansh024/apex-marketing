# Plastic-surgeon industry page: hero copy directions

Page: https://apex-marketing.ai/plastic-surgeon-industry/
Scope: copy only. The hero's layout, colours, video, calendar and ticker stay exactly as they are.

## Who is reading

The reader is the owner-surgeon or the practice/med-spa owner. This is not a marketing manager.

- Runs a $1–10M practice. High-ticket procedures (breast, BBL, rhinoplasty, facelift, body contouring), plus injectables and med-spa services that fill the gaps.
- Has already hired at least one agency. Got a monthly PDF of impressions and CPCs, and a front desk chasing "leads" who never answer, price-shop, or no-show.
- Measures the business in **consults booked → surgeries scheduled**, not in clicks. Knows exactly what an empty consult slot costs.
- Skeptical, short on time, and reads the hero on a phone between cases. Decides in about five seconds whether "these people get my practice."

## The test (Harry Dry / Seth Godin)

- **Can I picture it?** A consult calendar filling up. The hero already shows one.
- **Can it be proven wrong?** Name a number or an outcome someone could check, not an adjective. "Engineered to deliver results" fails.
- **Could only we say it?** It has to be tied to Apex's actual model: reporting in booked appointments, month-to-month terms, 60-day money-back guarantee.
- **Godin:** speak to the smallest viable audience ("people like us"). Name the tension the reader already feels, and don't try to sound impressive to everyone.

## Directions

### A: The ledger (contrast)
- Eyebrow: For plastic surgeons & med spas
- H1: **Booked consults.** Not more leads.
- Sub: Leads don't pay for surgery. Patients do. We fill your consult calendar, and it's the only number we report.

### B: The enemy (tension, Godin)
- Eyebrow: For surgeons burned by agencies
- H1: **Your last agency** sent you a report. We send patients.
- Sub: Meta, Google and local ads built for plastic surgery practices, measured in consults on your calendar.

### C: The promise (falsifiable)
- Eyebrow: Plastic surgery & med spa marketing
- H1: **More consults in 60 days,** or every dollar back.
- Sub: Omni-channel campaigns for plastic surgeons and med spas. Month-to-month. Written guarantee.

### D: The scene (visual, Dry)
- Eyebrow: For plastic surgeons & med spas
- H1: **Your next patient** is scrolling Instagram right now.
- Sub: We find them, qualify them and put them on your consult calendar before a competitor does.

### E: The front desk (specific pain)
- Eyebrow: For plastic surgeons & med spas
- H1: **Stop paying** for leads your front desk can't book.
- Sub: We qualify, nurture and schedule, then report one number: consults booked.

## Pick: A, the ledger

"Booked consults. Not more leads." sets up a fight the reader has already lost with another agency, and the animated calendar next to it proves the point without another word. The sub adds the Godin tension ("Leads don't pay for surgery. Patients do.") and ends on something that can be checked: *the only number we report.* The 60-day guarantee stays where it already works, in the trust chip and the certificate section, so the headline doesn't have to carry two promises.
